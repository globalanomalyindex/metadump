## FLUX.2 prompt

Product shot of wireless over-ear headphones made of transparent blue acrylic, with translucent blue vinyl cushions, on a white plinth in hyperreal electric-green grass under a deep, saturated blue sky. The color lives inside the material: ear cups are thin shells, pale clear blue across the face, deep sapphire at their glowing edge-on rims; the solid headband deepest at its thickest, darker where it crosses a cup. Hinges in the same acrylic, surfaces unmarked, crisp white highlights tracing every curve. A high sun behind the headphones shines through them, laying a blue shadow and bright caustics on the plinth and grass; backlit blade tips glow. Overexposed blue-tinted whites bloom around highlights and small cloud tops while sky and grass stay saturated. Low camera, framed against the sky. Frutiger Aero and Y2K translucency.

Aspect: 4:5

## Notes

1. The ear cups are treated as hollow shells: pale across the face, deepest along the rim where the wall is seen edge-on. A solid block would be deepest in the middle instead. The headband is solid, so it follows the solid rule. In a test render, if the cups come out one even blue, that is the painted-on failure. Also check that the shadow on the plinth is blue, not grey.
2. FLUX.2 has no negative prompt, so every exclusion is written as something positive. "Hinges in the same acrylic" heads off chrome or metal hardware, "surfaces unmarked" heads off logos and lettering, and "while sky and grass stay saturated" keeps "overexposed" from washing the image out. Don't add a "no ..." list, because naming a thing puts it into the image.
3. Free to change: the aspect (4:5 suits a single object; use 3:2 for more field), the white plinth (it gives the blue light somewhere to land, but grass alone works), and the camera angle. Keep real references (Bliss, Windows, iMac, Apple) out of the prompt so the result stays original.
