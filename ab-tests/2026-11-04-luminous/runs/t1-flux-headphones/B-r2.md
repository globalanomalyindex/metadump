## FLUX.2 prompt

Unbranded wireless over-ear headphones made entirely of transparent blue acrylic and translucent blue vinyl, the color carried inside the material, photographed outdoors standing on a low matte white plinth in hyperreal electric-green grass under a deep, saturated blue sky. Soft, rounded, slightly swollen forms with smooth, unmarked surfaces. The headband is one solid arc of clear cast acrylic: pale blue #B9E0FF where it tapers, deep sapphire #0A43B0 through its thickest curve, its polished cut edges the most saturated, brightest blue in the frame. The oval ear cups are hollow acrylic shells, clear and pale across their faces and deepest blue along their silhouettes, with the round drivers and fine internal ribs visible inside as softly refracted, darker-blue shapes. The ear cushions are plump pillows of translucent blue vinyl, clear at the center and deep blue around their rounded edges, with long soft highlights wrinkling along the seams. The sliders and hinges are the same clear blue acrylic, and wherever two parts overlap the blue turns darker and deeper. Crisp white highlights sit on top of every curve with the blue body visible beneath them, and bright reflective rims trace the sides.

A high late-morning sun stands three-quarters behind the headphones and shines through them toward the camera, laying a transparent blue shadow across the plinth top, threaded with bright caustic lines and pools of light blue #8FC8FF; the white plinth glows pale blue wherever that light lands. Overexposed blue-tinted whites bloom softly around the highlight cores, the plinth's sunlit edge and two small fair-weather clouds, while the sky holds full saturation, cobalt #1F64D4 overhead clearing to light blue at the horizon. Dense real lawn, fully green, backlit blade tips lit yellow-green by the sun, deep green between the blades, each blade crisp in the focal plane. Low camera at plinth height, 100mm lens at f/8, the headphones framed against open sky with the sky seen through the acrylic, horizon a third of the way up the frame, calm wide fields of sky and grass around one rich zone of refraction and caustics. Clear neutral daylight, perfectly clear air, crisp edges throughout. Frutiger Aero and Y2K translucency.

## Notes

1. FLUX.2 has no negative prompt, so every exclusion is written as the thing to show instead: painted-on color becomes a tint that deepens with thickness, grey shadows become a blue caustic shadow, chrome trim becomes blue acrylic hinges, haze becomes clear air, logos become unmarked surfaces. The headphones and their transparent blue body come first because FLUX.2 pays most attention to the opening words. The prompt uses no quality-tag filler and names no real products or wallpapers.
2. The hex values are starting points, not measured colors. They keep the blue between azure and cobalt and away from aqua. Loosen them if they over-steer. The aspect ratio is 4:5 for a single product; use 3:2 to show more lawn and sky.
3. How each part takes the color is my own reading of how the material behaves, not something the client said. The solid headband is deepest through its thickest arc. The hollow cups and the vinyl cushions are deepest along their outlines. Check it with a test render. If the cups come out one flat blue, fix that first.
