NOTES

1. The "inside the material" quality comes from the light-transport terms (light passing through, caustics, subsurface scattering, internal glow), and the negatives "painted surface / opaque plastic / colored film" protect it. If the panels come out opaque, raise the acrylic weight to 1.3 rather than adding more color words.
2. Suggested settings: 1216x832, DPM++ 2M Karras, 30-40 steps, CFG 5-6.5. Higher CFG tends to make the blues look painted on and clips the overexposed whites to flat white.
3. If the whites go pure white instead of blue-tinted, move "blue-tinted" ahead of "overexposed" or drop the bloom phrase. Skip the refiner, or keep it at 0.2 denoise or lower, because it can wash out the glow and the electric green.
