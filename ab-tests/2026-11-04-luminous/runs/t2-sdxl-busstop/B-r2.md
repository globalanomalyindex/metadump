NOTES

1. Settings: SDXL base at 1216x832 (3:2 landscape, the default for grass-and-sky scenes), CFG around 5-7. A high CFG tends to flatten the acrylic into one solid blue, which is exactly the painted-on look.
2. Keep the negative short. The positive already turns each exclusion into what you want instead (pale-to-sapphire depth, a blue shadow, a sky that holds its color), and negatives applied early can pull the unwanted thing into the image. If the panels still come out flat or frosted, add weight to the depth cue, e.g. `(deep sapphire where panels overlap:1.2)`, instead of adding more negatives.
3. "bus" is left out of the negative on purpose so it doesn't fight "bus stop"; "empty" plus "people" keep the scene quiet. The bus shelter's real-world ad panels and green-edged glass are why "advertisement" and "turquoise glass" made the list.
