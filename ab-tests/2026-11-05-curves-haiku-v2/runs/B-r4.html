Sticky and punchy curves

Three cubic bezier timing curves, each run as a loop: forward on its curve, then back. The graph playhead and the dot share one clock.

Presspunchy

Fast attack with a short kick. Most of the change lands in the first frames, progress peaks just past the target, and it clears quickly.

. .

Releasesticky

Grip, then release. A slow first fifth holds the start back before the middle lets go. No overshoot, so it lands clean.

. .

Catchsticky

Overshoot that snaps back and holds. Progress passes the target mid-curve, returns in the last third, and then rests on the mark.

. .

Which reading each curve carries is a choice; these three take one each: Press for punchy, Release and Catch for sticky.

Dashed grey curve: ease-out, for reference. Dashed line: the target. The dot passing its mark and returning is the overshoot.
