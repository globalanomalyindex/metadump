Sticky and punchy

Three custom cubic-bezier curves. Each one leaves fast, and each one lands and holds. Every loop plays the move, holds at rest, then resets.

Overshoot and settle figures are measured on the move leg of each curve (20,000 samples). The stop is judged at the 1 percent band: the point after which the curve stays within 1 percent of rest. The hold between loops is 1.2 s and lives in the loop, not in the timing function.

With reduced motion on, nothing loops. Each card shows its rest state and a button plays one move.
