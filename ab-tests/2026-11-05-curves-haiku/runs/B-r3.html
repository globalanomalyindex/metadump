Sticky and punchy curves

Three cubic-bezier curves, each looped as one move, a held rest, then a reset. Punchy is the fast leave and the visible impact. Sticky is the landing that holds with no drift after it.

Each loop: move on its curve, hold at rest for 1.44 s, reset. The graph marker tracks the curve and the readout shows its eased progress. With reduced motion on, nothing loops: the dot rests at its end and Play once runs one move.
