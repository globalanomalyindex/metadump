Three curves: sticky and punchy

Each curve leaves fast (punchy) and lands early into a held rest (sticky). Every loop plays the move at the curve's own duration, holds at rest for 1.4 s, then resets. The marker on the graph and the dot run on the same clock.

With reduced motion on, the loops stop and each dot sits at its rest position. Use "Play once" on a card to see one move. Stop check: each curve is within 1 percent of rest by about 0.65 of its run.
