# Rare Clouds

High above the ordinary blue,
where few have looked and fewer stay,
the rarest clouds drift into view
and paint their colors out of day.

The nacreous sheet at dusk gleams pearl,
a silver fire on a winter sea,
then the noctilucent veil, a swirl
of blue that glows when the sun is free.

Lenticular, they stack like plates,
still in winds that never still them.
Mammatus hang like heavy weights,
then melt before the eye can fill them.

So tell me, have you ever looked up
and caught a cloud you could not name,
and felt the sky, for one bright moment, stop?
