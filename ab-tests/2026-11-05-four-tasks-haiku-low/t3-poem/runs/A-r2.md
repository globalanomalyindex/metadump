**Rare Clouds**

Not the common cotton drifting by,
but nacreous ones that bloom at dusk,
their colors pooled like oil on glass,
then gone before the stars come up.

Noctilucent veils, silver-thin,
stitch the edge of the upper sky,
while lenticular discs hold still
above the peaks as if they'd stopped.

So if you catch one, rare and bright,
will you stop to watch it fade?
