# Rare Clouds

Above the everyday gray and white,
a few strange clouds drift into sight:
the nacreous ones that shimmer rose and green,
the mammatus hanging like a hive of pearl,
the lenticular stacked like a spinning coin,
the noctilucent glowing silver, thin and far.

They rarely stay, and never wait for us,
a few brief minutes, then they're gone without a fuss.

So when you catch one drifting, rare and bright,
will you stop to name it, or let it slip from sight?
