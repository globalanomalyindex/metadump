Process

Three firings, one studio, and a slow glaze that takes about a week to dry through.

Throw

Each form is thrown on the wheel and left to firm up for two days before trimming.

Bisque

A first firing hardens the clay so it can be dipped and handled without breaking.

Glaze

Poured, wiped and fired a second time at cone 6 until the surface settles to its colour.

Curve lab

Change the reading and the tester answers. Each change is checked against the practical limits: a settle under 400 ms, a return under 2 percent, and a label that stays legible.
