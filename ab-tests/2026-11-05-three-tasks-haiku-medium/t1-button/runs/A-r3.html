A quieter place for your research.

Lumen Notes gathers sources, highlights and half-formed ideas into one calm workspace, then helps you find the thread between them. Your notes stay yours, offline included.

  1. 1Clip a passage, a PDF page or a video timestamp. Lumen keeps the source attached.
  2. 2Write loose thoughts beside the clips. Nothing needs a title or a folder.
  3. 3Ask for the threads. Lumen shows which notes point the same way, with links back to each source.

Trials take about a minute to start. Cancel anytime.
