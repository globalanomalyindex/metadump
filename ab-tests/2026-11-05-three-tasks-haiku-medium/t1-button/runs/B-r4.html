Plan the trip you keep putting off.

Harbor & Pine turns a loose idea into a route with stops, rest days and a budget you can keep. Start with one place and add the rest later.

Sample: Douro Valley, 6 days
  1. Day 1: Porto, slow walk down to the river, dinner in Ribeira.
  2. Day 2: Train up the valley, check into a quinta with a terrace.
  3. Day 3: Rest day. Tasting at two small estates, no more than two.
  4. Day 4: Boat from Régua to Pinhão and back, whichever leaves first.
  5. Day 5: Free morning, a long lunch, the afternoon train.
  6. Day 6: Return to Porto, keep the last evening empty.
