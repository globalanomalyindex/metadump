# Verified candidates for N-item tasks, and constraints are not narrowings

A/B test 10 (`ab-tests/2026-11-05-four-tasks-sonnet/results.md`) built four tasks with Sonnet 5.5 at low effort, graded blind by Haiku. The arm with a lite 0.7.0 dump scored higher on all four (button 7.25 vs 6.75, haiku 7.67 vs 6.08, toast 7.83 vs 6.12, playlist 4.75 vs 4.17; pooled 64 wins, 17 losses and 15 ties, p = 0.0009). Three failures sat under the wins:

- **The playlist had nothing to pick from.** The prompt asked for "a 5-song playlist" of "lesser known soviet-era songs with melodious guitar riffs". The dump's research reached only band-level sources, and its pools named bands per reading and no songs. Every dump build recommended five lesser-known acts instead of five songs, and said it wouldn't invent titles it couldn't verify. The prompt-only builds named songs, and the graders' searches found them famous or fabricated. The dump side won obscurity (7.92 vs 3.67) and lost helpfulness (3.83 vs 4.75).
- **Verify called constraints narrowings.** All five recent builds (test 9's and the four in test 10) ran a widening round. Verify's round-2 "narrowings" were mostly scoped anti-keywords that already had an `instead`: 4 of 4 in the haiku dump, most of 9 in the playlist dump, a lexicon entry and anti-keywords in the toast dump. ADR 0005 says the user's words and constraint mechanisms may exclude. finalize's `narrowing_language` already exempted them; verify did not. Builds took 18 to 27 minutes each.
- **A contradiction with the user's words became an open question.** In the button dump, verify found that "always-on or looping motion at rest pulls against the prompt's 'hover state animations'". The main loop filed it as an `open_questions` default (`oq-rest-motion`), as the procedure told it to (`create.md` §7; the Workflow script's `next`). No builder acted on it. Graders marked down the always-on halos, and the dump side's practical score barely moved (6.83 vs 6.5).

We decided:
- **A task that asks for N existing items gets a pool of verified candidates.** Its dump holds more than N real items, at least 2N where the catalogs allow. Each is a `kind: "candidate"` facet (`term`, `by`, `year` or `version`, `reading`, `src`, `text`), confirmed by a fetched source and labeled by the quality reading it serves. The consumer composes the N. This is ADR 0010's "exemplars per reading" made concrete for items: a verified pool larger than N is wanted, and only a finished set of N is the deliverable.
  - Charting names the catalogs where such items are listed (discographies, databases, bibliographies, package registries) as scrape and research targets, and discovery aims at them.
  - A candidate no fetched source confirms is `status: "unverified"`, `prov: "inferred"`, tier 4: a lead, never a recommendation. finalize puts every candidate that isn't collected there, whatever it claimed (`candidate_unverified`), and warns when fewer than 5 candidates are verified (`candidate_pool_thin`). 5 is a floor, because finalize can't read N from the prompt.
  - Verify's job pass reports a pool with fewer than N verified candidates as a research gap, which earns even a lite build its one widening round, so a research ticket goes back to the catalogs. A consumer left with fewer than N says so to the user instead of inventing the rest.
- **Constraint mechanisms are not narrowings.** Verify's narrowing pass skips the user's words, scoped anti-keywords with an `instead`, invariants with their `variable`, and forbidden shortcuts. It reports an anti-keyword only when its scope is global and it rules out a whole scope or quality reading, and then it names that reading. Every narrowing carries a `kind` (`scope`, `quality`, `job`, `definition`, `copy`). The Workflow script drops a finding verify labels `constraint` (its label when unsure), and the gap planner drops, with a note, any constraint finding that slipped through. A widening round runs only for narrowings, contradictions against tier-1 collected entries, missing job steps, missing prompt clauses or a research gap. At lite, narrowings and research gaps earn its one widening round; contradictions go to the main loop. At standard and deep, tier-1/2 guesses still earn a gap round.
- **A contradiction against the user's words becomes a constraint, not an open question.** When a verify contradiction's `against_id` is a tier-1 `user_stated` entry, the gap round writes a scoped `not_wanted` anti-keyword with an `instead` that keeps the user's words. For the button: "motion running at rest", instead "animate on hover and focus; the rest state is still". Consumers pull anti-keywords with every slice; nobody reads an open question's default as a constraint. When no round is left for it (at lite a contradiction doesn't earn one), the main loop writes the anti-keyword itself. A contradiction against any other collected entry still gets its value replaced or moved to the options area. In use mode, when the user's words name a practical constraint (practical, accessible, compact, performant, cheap), the consumer runs the checks scoped to it before finishing: still review prompts, not gates (ADR 0003), but not skipped.

We rejected:
- holding a finished playlist of N in the dump, because ADR 0010 showed that consumers paste the set they're handed and the builds come out alike;
- letting consumers name items from their own knowledge, because that is what the prompt-only builds did, and the graders found the titles famous or made up;
- keeping ADR 0010's one or two exemplars per reading for item tasks, because test 10's pool named bands per reading and no songs, and every build that used it declined the literal ask;
- dropping verify's narrowing pass, because it caught the real narrowings in tests 3 and 8;
- leaving the exemption to finalize alone, because verify's findings decide whether a widening round runs, and finalize can't judge which reading an entry rules out;
- making the checks for a practical constraint gates, because ADR 0003's over-compliance failure would come back: the user's word makes the check worth running, not binding;
- keeping contradictions with the user's words as open questions with a stronger default, because a default settles a parameter (ADR 0010), and no builder read this one as a constraint;
- giving contradictions lite's widening round too, because the main loop turns the ones against the user's words into anti-keywords without another round, and lite stays one round unless the dump narrowed the request or lacks the items it was asked for.

## Consequences

- Dumps for "recommend N things" tasks cost more research: catalogs to scrape and a confirming quote per item. They hold real titles with sources, and a consumer can say honestly when there aren't enough.
- Widening rounds run less often, since constraint findings no longer earn them. Builds should get shorter than test 10's 18 to 27 minutes; a retest will say.
- `open_questions` shrinks: contradictions with the user's words land in `anti_keywords` instead.

The rest of 0.7.1 is tooling that follows from the same test. Each shard's budget gives about how many tokens its entries take, next to the facet count (lite research and specialists about 1,200), because test 10's lite dumps ran 30K to 47K tokens against 8–15K with `oversized_entry` firing 2 to 23 times per dump; `build` and `merge` now name every oversized facet by the shard that wrote it. The moodboard describes at most 8 images that bear on the task and lists the rest as skipped by id with a reason, which finalize counts as handled, and `images.py collect` takes 4 images per page instead of 12 (the button dump had 31 moodboard entries, mostly page screenshots and off-topic thumbnails). finalize checks each source's quote against the page `webpages.py` cached for its url (`quote_not_in_page`), and research and scrape take their quotes from those chunks, because three builders said many `researched` quotes came from WebFetch summaries or search snippets; a fact known only from those is `inferred`. Gap tickets that widen or rewrite get their own type, `gap`, with a specialist's provenance ceiling and normalize's right to `_replace` and `_delete`, because gap shards typed `specialist` couldn't edit and each widening round needed an extra normalize agent. Gap shards always carry the round they feed (`6N-gap-<section>-rN`), and finalize merges shards in round order, with `95-synthesis` and `97-repair` last: in filename order a `6N-gap-*` shard sorted before round 1's digest (`90-normalize`), which overwrote its edits on every merge.
